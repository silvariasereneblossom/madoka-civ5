# Madoka Magica: Wish for the World (revival) — overview

A revival of Vicevirtuoso's Civilization V mod Madoka Magica: Wish for the World (Workshop 273118128) on a bundled custom build of the Community Patch DLL: bug fixes, a mechanics rework of the ten Magical Girl civilizations, new girls, and a Lekmod-compatible multiplayer patch.
